<h2 id="s6">6. Photos and location</h2>

<strong>Photos.</strong> Photos can contain metadata (EXIF), for example the time and position of the shot. On your
device we can use it to suggest a place and time for the form. We remove the metadata from the copies we show publicly.
The originals are private.

<strong>Report location.</strong> We never store the raw GPS point. When you save, we randomly move the point you mark
by 20 to 30 m: first on your device, and again on our server. The shift is made once and does not change between views.
The stored point is therefore approximate and only you can see it (labelled "Approximate location · visible only to
you"). The public sees a wider area calculated from that point: at least 500 m radius for a lost animal, at least 150 m
for a found or sighted one. We do not write coordinates to logs. 30 days after a report closes, we blur the stored point
further, so only the blurred point and the area name remain.

<strong>Device location.</strong> We use your device location only when you choose to, for example to set the place of a
report. We do not track or store your device location; we store only the shifted report point you confirm.

<strong>Alerts when you are nearby (mobile app).</strong> If you turn this feature on and allow the app to use your
location in the background, your phone itself checks whether you have entered an approximate area where a lost pet was
last seen. These areas are public and approximate. The check happens only on your phone: your location is never sent to
us or to anyone else. You can turn the feature off at any time in the app settings, and we then remove all areas from
your phone.

<strong>Data on your phone.</strong> So that the app also works offline, it keeps public reports from your area on your
phone: approximate areas and small photos, for at most 7 days. It never keeps report points, chip numbers, contact
details or chats. When you sign out, we delete this data.

<strong>Map and place search.</strong> Your browser or app loads map tiles directly from OpenFreeMap servers. They can
see your IP address and use no cookies. Place search and the area name for a point on the map run on our own servers,
using open data from the Surveying and Mapping Authority of the Republic of Slovenia. We send the search text and the
point to no one else and do not store them.

<h2 id="s7">7. Reports without an account</h2>

You can report a found or sighted animal without an account. This creates an anonymous session. When you submit the
report you receive a private link that you use to manage the report and to chat. We store only a hash of the link,
not the link itself. For that reason we cannot restore it unless you optionally entered an email address.

To limit abuse we keep a random device identifier and a hashed IP address. We delete the hashed IP address after 48
hours. You can delete the report and its data at any time through the private link.

<h2 id="s8">8. Matching, artificial intelligence and automatic hiding</h2>

<h3>Matching</h3>

When you publish a report, we compare it with other reports: by chip number, area, time and the animal's attributes.
Later we will also compare how similar the photos are. The result is only a suggestion. The person looking for the
animal decides: they confirm or reject the suggestion. A suggestion has no legal or similarly significant effect on
anyone.

<h3>AI assistance</h3>

<p>When this feature is switched on, we will send the photos you upload to a report or pet profile to two providers:</p>

<ul>
	<li>
		<strong>Anthropic PBC (USA)</strong>, the Claude model with image reading: suggests species, breed, colours,
		size, collar and distinguishing marks, and gives a safety signal about the content of the image.
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>Voyage AI (USA)</strong>: computes a numerical representation of the image (a vector) that we use to
		find visually similar photos.
	</li>
</ul>

We do not send the providers any user identifiers, EXIF metadata or location data. The results are suggestions: you
confirm or correct them before publishing. We store the result against a hash of the image so that we do not process
the same image twice. Images flagged by the safety signal are held for review by human moderators before
publication.

<h3>Automatic temporary hiding</h3>

If three independent people report the same listing for abuse, we hide it temporarily until a moderator reviews it.
The moderator then decides whether to restore or remove it. If you think a report was hidden unnecessarily, write to
info@signapps.si. A person will review the decision again.

<strong>Current status.</strong> Payments in Najdimo are at present in test mode only. No real money moves and no card
is charged. These chapters on payments apply once we enable live payments.

<strong>How a payment works.</strong> Payments are processed by Stripe Payments Europe, Limited (Ireland) through Stripe
Checkout. When you pay, we redirect you to Stripe's page. We never see or store your card details. We keep the payment
ID, purpose, amount, status and timestamps. For part of the processing (for example fraud prevention and legal
obligations) Stripe is an independent controller and its own privacy policy applies.

<strong>What is always free.</strong> Reporting a lost, found or sighted animal, the map and alerts, matching (chip,
photo, attributes), chat, the printable PDF poster with a QR code, sharing and chip lookup are free for everyone,
forever. Shelters and municipalities use everything for free. Paid options never restrict this. The QR code on the
poster leads only to the public listing.

<strong>What you can pay for.</strong> Now: a thank-you contribution after a reunion with your animal (at least €2) and
an optional contribution after reporting a lost animal (€2–5). Later: a boost for a report's visibility, the Premium
Protect subscription and the monthly supporter plan Podpornik. Vet clinics can become a paying Vet Partner (below).

<strong>Rewards for finders.</strong> We do not pay out rewards and do not handle payments between users. If an owner
states in a report that they offer a reward, that statement is part of the report (chapter 3). The owner and the finder
arrange any reward between themselves, outside Najdimo, so we collect no payment data about it.

<p>Contributions are voluntary payments to a company to help run the service. They are not donations.</p>

<h2 id="s11">11. Ad counting</h2>

Ads are always labelled "Ad". There are no ads in the report-lost and report-found flows, at sign-in, in chat or at
payment.

We count impressions and clicks in our own database, aggregated by ad, ad slot, day and region. We use no cookies,
store nothing on your device and record no identifiers that could reveal who you are. For that reason we do not need
consent for this counting. We choose an ad by context, for example the region and species, not by who you are.

A click goes through our redirect to the advertiser's page. From there the advertiser's rules apply. Advertisers
receive only aggregated reports from us (impressions and clicks).
