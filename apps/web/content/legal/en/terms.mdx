<h2 id="s1">1. In short</h2>

Najdimo helps lost animals get home again. Everything that brings animals home is free for everyone and will stay
that way. To do that we need a few rules.

<ul>
	<li>Post only genuine reports of dogs and cats.</li>
	<li>Never send money in advance. Collect an animal in a public place.</li>
	<li>Match suggestions and AI suggestions can be wrong. We do not promise that you will find your animal.</li>
	<li>We may hide or remove reports that break the rules.</li>
	<li>Paid options are never required and never limit the search.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="s2">2. What Najdimo is</h2>

Najdimo is a website and mobile app for posting and searching for lost, found and sighted dogs and cats in Slovenia.
The service is operated by the company SIGNAPPS, grafična in digitalna agencija, d.o.o., Kolomban 34B, 6280 Ankaran – Ancarano, Slovenija ("we").
"Najdimo" is the working name of the service.

<p>Our purpose is to get animals home. Money only keeps the service running. These are free for everyone, forever:</p>

<ul>
	<li>reporting a lost, found or sighted animal;</li>
	<li>the map and alerts;</li>
	<li>matching (chip number, photo, attributes);</li>
	<li>chat;</li>
	<li>the printable PDF poster with a QR code, and sharing;</li>
	<li>chip lookup.</li>
</ul>

<p>Paid options (Boost, Premium Protect, Podpornik, ads and Vet Partner) never limit these.</p>

<strong>What we do not promise.</strong> We cannot guarantee that you will find your animal or that a report will reach
a certain number of people. Match suggestions and attributes suggested by artificial intelligence can be wrong. Always
judge for yourself and check them before you publish.

<h2 id="s4">4. Accounts and reports without an account</h2>

<strong>Account.</strong> The information you enter must be correct. Keep your password and access safe. You are
responsible for what happens in your account. If you suspect misuse, tell us at once. You can delete your account at any

<strong>Reports without an account.</strong> You can report a found or sighted animal without an account. You receive a

<h2 id="s6">6. Safety and scams</h2>

<ul>
	<li>Never send money in advance. A genuine finder does not ask for money in advance.</li>
	<li>Collect or hand over an animal in a public place, in daylight, with someone you trust.</li>
	<li>Never share a code you receive by text message.</li>
	<li>Najdimo never asks you for a payment in a chat.</li>
	<li>Report suspicious behaviour with "Report abuse" or at info@signapps.si.</li>
</ul>

<strong>Reward.</strong> In a report of a lost animal, the owner may state that they offer a reward, with an amount or a
short note. The line may not contain contact details or ask for any payment in advance; we remove such lines. This is
the owner's statement, not ours. Najdimo does not hold, pay or guarantee rewards. The owner and the finder arrange any
reward between themselves, outside Najdimo. Never pay anyone anything in advance: a reward is handed over only once the
animal is home.
