<h2 id="s1">1. In short</h2>

Najdimo uses only a few cookies and similar storage items. Most are necessary for the service to work at all. We
switch on analytics only with your consent.

<ul>
	<li>
		Necessary items work without consent: your cookie choice, sign-in, language, report drafts and the private link
		to a report.
	</li>
	<li>
		Google Analytics sets the cookies <code>_ga</code> and <code>_ga_PKY4SCX3RL</code> only if you accept.
	</li>
	<li>
		There are no advertising or tracking cookies. We count ad impressions and clicks in aggregate and without
		cookies.
	</li>
	<li>
		You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through "Cookie settings" in the page footer or in the app
		settings.
	</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="s2">2. What cookies and similar storage are</h2>

A cookie is a small file that a website saves in your browser. We treat similar items (the browser's local storage,
secure storage in the app) the same way. In this text we call them all "items".

<p>We split items into two kinds:</p>

<ul>
	<li>
		<strong>necessary</strong>: without them the service does not work or cannot do what you asked for. We do not
		need consent for them;
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>analytics</strong>: they measure how the service is used. We set them only with your consent.
	</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="s5">5. What we do not use</h2>

<ul>
	<li>
		There are no advertising or tracking cookies. All four Google consent settings are denied by default. If you
		accept, we grant only <code>analytics_storage</code>.
	</li>
	<li>
		We count ad impressions and clicks in our own database, in aggregate and without cookies. We store nothing on
		your device for it.
	</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="s7">7. How your choice works</h2>

<ul>
	<li>
		Until you decide, we load no Google script, send no request to Google and set no Google cookie (basic Consent
		Mode v2).
	</li>
	<li>If you decline or choose nothing, the service works the same. Nothing is locked.</li>
	<li>
		If you accept, we grant only <code>analytics_storage</code>.
	</li>
	<li>We ask you again after 12 months or when providers or purposes change.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="s8">8. How to change or withdraw your choice</h2>

At any time, open "Cookie settings" in the page footer or in the app settings and change your choice. Withdrawal
takes effect at once, and we delete the <code>\_ga</code> cookies at that moment. You can also delete cookies in your
browser settings; necessary items are created again when the service needs them, but drafts are lost.

<h2 id="s9">9. Changes</h2>

We update this text when we add or remove an item or a provider. The date of the last update is at the top of the
page. For an important change we ask for your choice again.
