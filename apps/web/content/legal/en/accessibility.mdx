<h2 id="s1">1. In short</h2>

Najdimo should work for everyone, including under stress and on a small screen. Our target is conformance with WCAG
2.2 level AA and the European standard EN 301 549, as the European Accessibility Act (EAA) requires.

<strong>Status: partially conformant.</strong> An external audit in milestone M5 will confirm it. Until then this
statement is a self-assessment.

<h2 id="s2">2. What this statement covers</h2>

<ul>
	<li>the Najdimo website;</li>
	<li>the iOS mobile app;</li>
	<li>the Android mobile app.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="s3">3. What we already do</h2>

<ul>
	<li>Body text and the main action have a contrast of 7:1.</li>
	<li>Everything works with a keyboard and a screen reader.</li>
	<li>The map always has an equivalent list alternative.</li>
	<li>Photos have alt text.</li>
	<li>We respect the reduced-motion setting.</li>
	<li>You can enlarge text up to 200 % without breaking the content.</li>
	<li>Field labels sit above the fields, and errors are written as text, not shown by colour alone.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="s4">4. Known limitations</h2>

<ul>
	<li>
		<strong>The interactive map</strong> is a supplement. Everything on it is also in the list.
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>User photos</strong> may have only a description generated from the animal's attributes, because the
		author did not write one.
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>Third-party pages</strong> are outside our control: payment on Stripe Checkout, sign-in with Google or
		Apple, and the "not a robot" check (Cloudflare Turnstile).
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>PDF posters</strong> are tagged, but accessibility may vary between posters.
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>The admin portal and the shelter portal</strong> are designed mainly for desktop computers.
	</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="s5">5. How this statement was prepared</h2>

We prepared this statement on 15 October 2026. Method: self-assessment during development. An external audit is
planned in milestone M5. We will update the statement after the audit.
