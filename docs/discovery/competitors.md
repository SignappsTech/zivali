## C. Gaps in the SI market

1. No national, structured lost/found database. Information is scattered across 17 shelter sites, dozens of FB groups and one radio slot.
2. Shelter found lists lack the found location and time, so nobody can match them, by hand or automatically.
3. Cats are the real problem: about 90 % of shelter lost and found listings, mostly unchipped until the 2027 obligation.
4. No public chip lookup since June 2026.
5. Contact numbers are published openly (FB, radio), and nothing protects owners from scams.
6. Nobody tells people which shelter is responsible for their municipality, though by law that is the one to call.

## D. Patterns to reuse and pitfalls (from A and B)

**Reuse**
- Proxy contact on posters (TASSO/FINDEFIX; already in R-011.1).
- Found sub-types *in possession / sighting* (already FOUND vs SIGHTED); consider *deceased* (moderated, no public photo).
- Shelter auto-ingest with auto-removal on outcome.
- "Still missing?" check-ins (already FR-012).
- Pre-registered pet profiles.
- One-off pricing with auto-stop on reunion.
- Alert vets and shelters within X km.
- Yearly public data report for PR, SEO and the Partners page.
- Media partner (radio).
- Insurer-paid boosts.

**Avoid**
- Paywalled safety (Pet Alert).
- Forced finder accounts (PawBoost, Petco, DogLost).
- Weekly auto-renew (PawBoost complaints).
- Opt-out surveillance (Ring).
- FB-only dependence.
- Unverifiable success claims.
- 24/7 call centres (cost).

**Where Najdimo is unique in SI and among the references**
- No-account found report in < 60 s.
- Photo AI matching free for all (Petco is US-only; Pet Alert keeps it Premium).
- Shelter intake matching for SI.
- Offline cache and offline found-report queue.
- **Geofence proximity alerts**: no reference product does on-device, opt-in "you are near where X was last seen". Ring's camera search is the closest, and it is opt-out and dogs-only.
