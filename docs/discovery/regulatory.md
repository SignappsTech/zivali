# Regulatory facts to verify (Slovenia)

**Read this first**
- This is a researcher's summary of primary texts (Uradni list RS amendment texts, gov.si) plus a few secondary sources. It is not legal advice.
- The official consolidated text of ZZZiv on PISRS could not be machine-read (it is a single-page app). Consolidated wording therefore comes from the amendment acts themselves or from secondary portals.
- **Before relying on any row, a lawyer must check the current consolidated text on pisrs.si.**
- Confidence: **H** = read in an official amendment or primary text; **M** = primary text but possibly amended later, or an interpretation of it; **L** = secondary source or an inference.

Abbreviations: ZZZiv = Zakon o zaščiti živali (Ur. l. RS 38/13 UPB, 21/18 ZNOrg, 92/20 ZZZiv-D, 159/21 ZZZiv-E, 109/23 ZZZiv-F, 12/25 odl. US, 60/25 ZZZiv-G). CRHŽ = centralni register hišnih živali (the former "centralni register psov", CRPsi, renamed by ZZZiv-E in 2021). UVHVVR = Uprava za varno hrano, veterinarstvo in varstvo rastlin.

## Vet Partner placements: is ad labelling required? (ZVPot-1 / UCPD)

Placements in question (brief v0.9, FR-028 R-028.5): a paid clinic shown first in the **"nearest vet" slot** (detail page, reunited screen), a **"Need help? Nearest vet: …" line on posters** (the owner may remove it) and **in lost-pet alerts**, and a verified clinic profile.

**Short answer: yes.** Every paid placement must be recognisable as advertising, and the word "nearest" must be true or dropped. The law sets no fixed wording, but "**Oglas**" (already R-030.1) satisfies it.

**Recommended rules (input for FR-028, FR-030 and the content slices):**
1. Every paid placement is labelled **"Oglas"** (EN "Ad"), visibly, on screen and on the printed poster, next to the clinic name, e.g. *"Oglas · Partnerska veterinarska ambulanta: Ambulanta X, 01 …"*.
2. Never use "najbližji" or "nearest" for a paid entry unless it is the geographically nearest open clinic. Otherwise say *"Veterinar v vaši regiji (oglas)"*.
3. If organic (non-paying) clinics are listed too, the paid one is marked even when it is shown first. Add a "Kako razvrščamo" link.
4. Posters: keep the owner's opt-out (v0.9). Show the owner before download that the line is paid.
5. Alerts: no paid line in email or push without marketing consent. In-app and posters only by default.
6. Vet Partner terms: ranking rules, labelling, the clinic's warranty of accurate hours and 24 h claims, and compliance with veterinary professional rules.

## Open points for the legal check

1. The current consolidated art. 30 ZZZiv: is the 8-day reclaim period still valid, and how does the "pisni poziv" (written call) work? (R-06)
2. Whether Pravilnik o zaščiti hišnih živali 5. člen (72 h owner, 24 h finder) is still in force and current. (R-03, R-04)
3. Can our app be named a channel for the legal reports (R-02, R-04)? Our assumption is no: the app must tell users to also report to the shelter or vet.
4. Whether a finder who keeps a found animal at home breaches R-04. This affects the copy in the found flow.
5. R-11: if any feature lets users or shelters advertise a change of ownership (adoption, "podarim"), we become an "ad-space provider" with a duty to verify.
6. Dohodnina partner model (D-05): wording, plus the VAT and accounting treatment of tips (Q-008).
