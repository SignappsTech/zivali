Brand and chosen direction for the lost-pet app.

Calm, warm, hopeful and trustworthy for people who are often frightened and in a hurry. Readable in direct sunlight, in light and dark, and clean enough that nothing competes with the one thing to do next.

Name

Slovenian first, still easy for an English reader, short, and no diacritics in the name itself so it works in domains and links. DNS lookups only show whether a name resolves today, not whether it is free or safe to trademark. A registry and trademark check is still needed.

Najdimo

Recommended

"Let's find (them)." A call to the whole community.

For: an active verb that matches the product (matching plus neighbours). Works for owners and finders. Seven letters, no diacritics. najdimo.si, .com, .app and .eu do not resolve today.

Against: not self-explanatory to English readers, so the tagline carries the meaning.

Tagline: Spet doma. / Home again.

Pot domov

"The way home." A path, like the sighting trail.

For: emotional, ties to the map path.

Against: two words, long in a URL, "pot" also means sweat, and it names the goal rather than the action.

Spet doma

"Home again." The happy ending as a name.

For: warm, hopeful.

Against: the English form collides with HomeAgain, an existing US pet-microchip brand. Better as the tagline.

Doma

"At home." The state we want to reach.

For: very short, gentle.

Against: an everyday word, so weak for search and trademarks. doma.si is already taken.

Sled

"Trail, trace." The tracking idea.

For: short, the trail fits sightings.

Against: reads as "sled" in English and sounds cold. sled.si is already taken.

Why not "Izgubljene živali"?

It is a descriptive working name that leads with loss and sounds like a registry. It stays as the SEO phrase: "Najdimo, izgubljene živali v Sloveniji".

Tone of voice

Calm, warm, hopeful, trustworthy. We speak like a kind neighbour who knows what to do, not like an alarm and not like a mascot.

We say

  • "Prijavite izgubo. Takoj obvestimo sosede v vaši bližini."
  • "Našli smo možno ujemanje. Preverite fotografijo."
  • "Telefonska številka ostane skrita."

We avoid

  • Alarm: capitals, many exclamation marks, red banners.
  • Cute talk, paw puns and emoji in the interface.
  • Guilt, pity, or promises we cannot keep.

Slovenian copy uses gender-neutral wording (nouns and imperatives such as "Prijavi izgubo") so it never guesses who is speaking.

Logo concept

A map pin with two soft ears: a place and a pet at once, so it reads as "a lost animal on a map" before any word does. The hole in the pin is the eye of the search and holds a sun-yellow dot, the one spark of hope.

  • Works at 16 px (favicon, map marker), in one colour plus the sun dot.
  • The ears are neutral enough for both cats and dogs.
  • The wordmark is live text in Bricolage Grotesque with tight tracking.

Najdimo

Direction A: Lagoon, kept calm

Cool teal and mist, soft shapes, sun yellow only for the logo dot and the reunited moment. After client review the screens were stripped back: one primary action per screen, one question at a time, no card borders, no shadows, status as an icon plus a coloured word.

Palette

7:1 for body text and the main action. Light and dark designed together.

Type

Bricolage Grotesque

Atkinson Hyperlegible Next

Full Slovenian diacritics. Body 16 px or more.

Shape and surface

Pill buttons, 24 px cards, 28 px sheets. Tone separates surfaces; no borders on cards, shadows only on floating overlays.

Fields are a label and a line.
