Lagoon, kept calm

Flat, quiet surfaces in cool teal and mist. One action per screen, one question at a time, and space instead of lines. Sun yellow stays in the logo and in the moment a pet is reunited.

  • Surfaces separate by tone, not by outline. Cards have no border and no shadow.
  • One primary action per view. Everything else is a quiet text button.
  • One clear heading level, generous spacing, body text at 16 px or more.
  • Readable in direct sunlight: 7:1 for body text and the main action, status never by colour alone.

Logo: pin with ears

A map pin that is also a pet. The hole is the eye of the search and holds the sun dot.

Palette

Hex and contrast change with the colour mode. Every pair is checked by the token build.

Type

Bricolage Grotesque for headings and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for text. Atkinson was drawn by the Braille Institute for low vision, which suits glare and stress. Both carry full Latin Extended, so Č, Š and Ž render natively.

Imagery

Honest, owner-snapshot photography: natural light, eye level, the animal fills the frame, faces readable. Photos sit in rounded frames with no labels on them. Illustration is flat geometry in two or three brand colours, only for empty states and onboarding. The photos here are placeholders.

Do

Real pets in real places. Ask for 3 to 5 angles. Crop square for rows, 4:3 for detail.

Avoid

Filters, text on photos, stock models, pity clichés, pills and bars over faces.

Generated images

Illustration and marketing only. Never for a real report.

Alt text

Species, colour, one distinguishing mark.
