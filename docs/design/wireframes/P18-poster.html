What changes against today (numbers on S2)

  1. Top band: an ink block (186 by 28 mm, corners 3 mm) with the status word in paper colour, Bricolage 800 in capitals at 64 pt, shrinking to fit down to 40 pt. Today: 80 pt black type on white. The status is still a word, never a colour. Outline variant: the same block with a 1.2 mm ink frame on paper.
  2. Photo: unchanged width (114 mm) and rule (takes the spare height, here 100 to 140 mm), corners 4 mm, cropped from the top so the head stays in (system §9 photo framing). Today it is cropped from the centre.
  3. Scan column (64 mm): the question in 26 pt display at the top ("Ste ga videli?"), the action in 14 pt under it with a down arrow, the QR (56 mm, ink on paper) at the bottom with 8 mm clear above and 8.5 mm below it (the 4-module quiet zone), level with the photo's lower edge. Same poster.cta_* string, split at the first "? ".
  4. Short link: "najdimo.si/r/" at 14 pt with the code in 19 pt extra-bold, letter-spaced. Atkinson Hyperlegible keeps 0/O and 1/l apart, so the code can be typed from the street.
  5. Name: 72 pt on one line, shrinking to 56 pt, then two lines at 56 pt. If the page would still run over, the photo gives up height first (never below the scan column), then the name shrinks, down to 44 pt (S5). Today it shrinks to 48 pt and then wraps.
  6. When and where: one line each, 20 pt, with a calendar and a map pin icon (the same two strings as today).
  7. Facts: a labelled row (BARVA over "rjava in bela", VELIKOST, PASMA) under a mid-grey hairline, up to three columns. Same poster.fact_* strings, split at ": ".
  8. Reward: an outlined box (0.6 mm ink, corners 3 mm): amount 24 pt bold with tabular figures, the owner's note at 14 pt on the same line, cut with "…" at the edge.
  9. Safety line: a shield icon in front, 12 pt, in ink (it is required text, so no grey).
  10. Footer: hairline, mark and "Najdimo · Spet doma." on the left, "najdimo.si" on the right.

No photo (S4): the photo's place becomes a description card: 0.6 mm ink frame, the species pictogram (Phosphor dog or cat, 32 mm) at the top and the facts stacked at 28 pt at the bottom; the fact row under the name is dropped. Found (S6): no name zone, the photo takes the space. Home again (S7): band "Spet doma", the photo alone at full width, the sentence and the thanks, no QR.

Print and greyscale rules (kept)

  • Ink #101A1D on paper #FFFFFF (17:1); the one mid-grey (light input, about 4.1:1) only for hairlines and the photo well, never for text.
  • QR at least 55 mm (56 mm), 4-module white quiet zone (the gap to the photo and the page margin keep it white), the link printed under it.
  • No phone number, email, chip number, collar text, exact address or holding place. Area names are the approximate ones.
  • The ink band and the outlines are fills in the PDF, so they print without "background graphics"; only this browser mock needs that print setting.
