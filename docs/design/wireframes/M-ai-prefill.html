Questions for the owner

  1. Filled or only highlighted? Shown: the AI values are selected in the fields and marked "Predlog"; your own Save or Publish confirms them (F4). The other way is the "suggested" pill style, where every value waits for a tap: safer, but slower for someone in a panic. Recommended: filled.
  2. Confidence without numbers? Shown: at 0.6 or more a value is filled and marked; below that the field stays empty with a hint (F3, F4). No percentages and no "maybe" level. OK?
  3. Nothing certain enough (F10): one line by the button, or a hint under every field (as C19 reads now)? Recommended: one line.
  4. Found flow sentence (F2): species, size and colour only (as shown), or add the breed ("Pes, labradorec, črne barve")?
  5. Another animal (F8): let the finder carry on by hand (as shown), or stop the report, because only dogs and cats can be reported?
  6. Several animals (F8): "Zamenjaj fotografijo" only (as shown), or do you want a crop tool in M3? It is extra work on web and mobile, and the copy would then say "crop it".
  7. Before saving (F7): a quiet line and "Preveri predloge" next to Save, never blocking (as shown). OK, or must the person confirm each suggested field one by one?

    Frames

    Ten frames, each with Desktop 1440 and Mobile 390. Mobile 390 is the app and the web at phone width (same layout). Some frames have a state switch.

    Rules this wireframe sets (go to P7 §AI suggestions after approval)

    1. One AI slot per screen, next to the main action (mobile footer above the primary, web above the buttons, P8 web in the summary card). It holds, in turn: the checking line, the AI note ("Predlog iz fotografije. Umetna inteligenca se lahko moti."), the check-before-saving line on the saving step, or a late notice. It sits below the fields, so changing it never moves a field.
    2. Predlog mark: sparkle (fill, 16) and "Predlog" 14 bold in primary, after the field label; where the h1 is the label (P7 step 2 edit), in the selected row. It is part of the field's accessible name. It goes away when the person changes the field, and on Save or Publish. "Da, nadaljuj" accepts the P7 sentence.
    3. Hints (low confidence, species differs, collar): sparkle outline 16 in primary and 14/20 muted-foreground text under the field. Never a boxed panel (system 10.8).
    4. Fill rule: only empty fields the person has not touched; a cleared suggestion counts as touched and never comes back. A field that still carries the mark counts as untouched when a replaced first photo is checked again.
    5. Late result: after the person touched a field on the current step, it only fills and marks; it adds no hint lines and never moves focus.
    6. Species differs: hint, never switched; breed codes of the other species are never filled (C19 drops them only against the model's own species).
    7. Notice: nothing is filled; the note shows on the photo step, or in the slot when the person has already moved on.
    8. Announcements: one polite live-region message per photo ("Predlogi iz fotografije so vpisani v {count} polja. Preverite jih.", plural forms per ICU); the checking line is role="status".
    9. Nothing saved unconfirmed: values become the person's own only through Save (P8), Objavi iskanje (P6), or "Da, nadaljuj" and Objavi prijavo (P7). Save is never blocked by remaining marks.

    Proposed contract changes, to file after approval: C19 §Client rules gets the species-differs fill rule (6), "no fill with a notice" (7) and, if question 3 is answered so, the one-line case (F10).
