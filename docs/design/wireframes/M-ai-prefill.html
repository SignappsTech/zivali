AI pre-fill in the pet, lost and found forms

Component mock of the photo and details steps where suggestions appear: P7 found flow (steps 1 and 2), P6 lost flow on the new-pet path (steps 2, 3 and 7) and the P8 pet form (web page and mobile steps). Built from the real flow screen, ChoiceRow, Pill, Select, Input, Note and PetFields specs with the values of docs/design/tokens.css. What the form gets from the API follows contract C19 (GET /v1/photos/:id/attributes).

Sample data is the C19 example: a black dog. Species dog 0.98, breed Labradorec 0.81, colour black 0.93, pattern solid 0.77, size below the 0.6 threshold (0.41), collar seen 0.88. In v2 the mark shows a confidence level: 0.85 or more is "zelo verjetno", 0.6 to 0.84 is "verjetno" (so species and colour read "zelo verjetno", breed and pattern "verjetno").

    Rules this wireframe sets

    Rules 1 to 10 were approved with v1 on 2026-10-19 and are in docs/design/pages/P7-report-found.md §AI suggestions. Rules 11 to 13 are new in v2 and wait for the owner.

    1. One AI slot per screen, next to the main action (mobile footer above the primary, web above the buttons, P8 web in the summary card). In turn it holds: ai_prefill.checking, then the note (note_continue in P6 and on P7's "Uredi podatke" path, note_save in P8) with the "Kako nastanejo predlogi?" text button, or a late notice. It sits below the fields, so changing it never moves a field. The copy slice places the note "above the filled fields"; this layout keeps it by the button because a late result would otherwise push the fields down. To settle in P7 §AI suggestions.
    2. Predlog mark (ai_prefill.suggested): sparkle (fill, 16) and "Predlog" 14 bold in primary, after the field label; where the h1 is the label (P7 "Uredi podatke"), in the selected row. The copy calls it a pill; it is drawn as a status word because system 10.5 keeps tonal pills to one per page. suggested_a11y is the field's accessible description. It goes when the person changes the field, and on Save or Publish. "Da, nadaljuj" accepts the P7 sentence. v2 adds the level to it (rule 11).
    3. Hints (low_confidence, low_confidence_breed, collar_hint, collar_hint_note, species_differs): sparkle outline 16 in primary and 14/20 muted-foreground text under the field. Never a boxed panel (system 10.8).
    4. Fill rule: only empty fields the person has not touched; a cleared suggestion counts as touched and never comes back. A field that still carries the mark counts as untouched when a replaced first photo is checked again.
    5. Late result: after the person touched a field on the current step, it only fills and marks; it adds no hint lines and never moves focus.
    6. Species differs: hint plus the species_switch text button; never switched by itself. Breed codes of the other species are never filled (C19 drops them only against the model's own species). Pressing "Izberi psa" is the person's choice; empty, untouched fields valid for the new species then fill and are marked.
    7. Notices: a neutral note under the first photo with "Zamenjaj fotografijo" and "Obdrži fotografijo" (dismisses it). no_animal and other_animal: nothing is filled and P7 skips "Je to pravilno?" (manual step with the notice above it). multiple_animals: fills as usual. If the person has already left the photo step, the notice shows in the slot.
    8. Announcements (one polite region per photo): arrived_live with the number actually filled, or none_live, or the notice title. Nothing when the person has filled every field already. The checking status clears without an announcement.
    9. Nothing saved unconfirmed: values become the person's own only through Save (P8), Objavi iskanje (P6), or "Da, nadaljuj" and Objavi prijavo (P7). Save is never blocked by remaining marks.
    10. Disclosure: "Kako nastanejo predlogi?" opens info_body inline on web and in a bottom sheet on mobile, with the privacy link to /zasebnost#s8. Shown only when ai.enabled is true.
    11. v2, confidence level (Q2): the mark reads "Predlog · zelo verjetno" (0.85 or more) or "Predlog · verjetno" (0.6 to 0.84). The level is 14 regular muted-foreground after the bold "Predlog" (in a selected row it takes the row's text colour). It is text, never colour alone, and never a number. The field's accessible description becomes "Predlog iz fotografije, zelo verjetno pravilen. Preverite ga." Below 0.6 nothing changes: empty field and the hint. Level and mark go together.
    12. v2, breed in the found sentence (Q4): report_found.ai_sentence becomes size, species, breed, colours: "Pes, labradorec, črne barve". The breed is left out when it is null, *.mixed or *.unknown. "Uredi podatke" then shows "Pasma (neobvezno)", filled and marked, so the finder can change or clear it. "Da, nadaljuj" makes the breed part of the report.
    13. v2, crop tool (Q6): opens from "Obreži fotografijo" in the several-animals note and from the crop button under any photo. A frame with four corner handles (44 px targets) and edge handles, dragged to move and resized from a corner. With the keyboard: Tab goes to the frame, then each corner; arrows move the frame or resize from the focused corner; Shift moves faster; Esc cancels. Mobile is a full screen, web a dialog. "Uporabi" replaces the photo in the form with the cropped copy (nothing is saved yet); for the first photo the check runs again (F12). "Prekliči" changes nothing. Focus goes back to the button that opened it. The library is the tech-lead's choice (ADR-017) and must give these keyboard and screen-reader basics.
