My payments (P15 W3): one row per payment, and a payment detail

Wireframe for the tester's report GitHub #12: on My payments the payments run together, and there is no way to see one payment. Proposal: every payment is its own card row, and the whole row opens a detail page with the amount, date, status, payment method and, where there is one, the receipt PDF. A component mock from the real tokens and the current list (apps/web/components/payments/payments-list.tsx, TASK-32.2), Desktop 1200 and Mobile 390, light by default. Design only: nothing is built until the owner approves.

Pilot rule: the receipt PDF is a mock until fiscalisation is cleared (FISCAL_PROVIDER=mock, C13 §Fiscal invoices), so the detail labels it as a test document. Fields come from C13 Payment; what the API does not have yet is marked needs API and listed as CHANGE rows CH-701 to CH-704. Sample data; today is 20. 10. 2026.

    Rules this wireframe sets

    1. One card row per payment (new "payment row", a navigation row in the choice-row style of system 10.4): card surface on background, radius 20, 12 px between rows, min height 76, no border, no divider, no shadow. Left: purpose (17 bold) over the date (14 muted). Right: amount (17 bold, tabular) over the status word. A 20 px caret at the end. Newest first, as today.
    2. The whole row is one link to the detail, at least 44 px high. Its name reads in order: purpose, date, amount, status. Hover: foreground at 10 % over the row. Focus: 3 px ring, 2 px offset. Pressed: scale(0.98).
    3. Status as built (system 10.5, icon + word): Plačano (check, primary icon), Neuspešno (warning, destructive), Vrnjeno and Delno vrnjeno (return arrow, muted-foreground). Failed and fully refunded amounts are muted: they cost nothing now. Never colour alone.
    4. Test mode once per view: a Note above the list, a Note in the detail, and a line in the receipt block. Shown from the payment's own flag (CH-702), not from the environment.
    5. Detail page in the same 640 px column: back link "Vsa plačila" (text button, 44 px), h1 = purpose, the amount large (display 48/56, 40/48 on a phone), the status word under it, then one card with the facts (date and time, payment method, paid and refunded amounts), then the "Potrdilo" section in its own card. Cards are never nested.
    6. Receipt: the number and "Prenesi potrdilo (PDF)" (secondary; full width on a phone) only when invoice.pdf_ready. Not ready yet: one sentence, plus the email line when receipt_sent_at is set. No invoice and no email: the section is left out. Failed payments have no section. The fiscal status (pending, verified, failed) is not shown to the user.
    7. Pilot: every receipt PDF is a mock. While invoice.test is true, the receipt block says it is a test document without fiscal verification.
    8. No actions on a payment beyond the download: no "pay again", no refund request, no ads (payment screens carry none). A failed payment says that nothing was charged.
    9. Lines that need missing data are left out, not shown empty: no method, no refund date, no email line while the field is null.
    10. Navigation: list to detail and back keep the list's scroll position; on the detail the route announcer reads the h1. Document title: "Plačilo 19. 10. 2026 · Najdimo" (content strategist names it).

    Not in this wireframe

    The app screens (TASK-32.3 follows the same structure once approved); the credit note (dobropis) PDF, which has no download route yet (C13); the receipt email and the PDF layout; a link to the report a payment was for (Q8); subscriptions and Boost (M4: they reuse the same row and detail, with the plan name as the purpose); grouping by month, which is not needed for a few voluntary payments and can come with M4.
