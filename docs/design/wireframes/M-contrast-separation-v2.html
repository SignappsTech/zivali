    Questions for the owner

    1. Fields as filled boxes (change 3): every text field, select, textarea and search becomes a box with the field fill and a 2 px input outline, radius 16, on web and in the app; choice rows (Pes / Mačka, situation, the Prijavi menu) get the same outline while unselected. OK? Recommended: yes. It answers "unclear which elements are inputs" and it also settles GH#3 (below).
    2. Edges (change 2): a 1 px border line on every card, sheet, dialog, menu, map frame and the top of sticky bars, in light and dark. OK? Recommended: yes, one rule for both themes.
    3. Section dividers (change 4): a 1 px line where each page section starts, with the same spacing as today. The alternative is alternating full-width tone bands, which need more layout work and fight with the cards. Recommended: dividers.
    4. Dark surfaces (change 1): cards night.650, dialogs, notes and the segmented track night.550; teal text and the primary button one step lighter (lagoon.250) so links keep 7:1; the closing band and the shelter-portal sidebar in deep teal (lagoon.900), as in light. OK? Recommended: yes.
    5. Tonal buttons and pills (change 5): light lagoon.200 instead of the faint lagoon.100 tint; dark lagoon.700 with a lagoon.50 label. OK? Recommended: yes. In v1 I left light alone; v2 changes it because the home button "Prijavi najdeno žival" is barely a shape in light today (1.05:1).
    6. Report detail on web (F6): the facts and actions on the right sit in one card. OK? Recommended: yes; it is the clearest group on that page.

    If something is still too weak or too strong, say which number (1 to 5) and which way; the values are one step apart on the scales, so a change is quick.

    GH#3, the textarea (TASK-1.3): decided as option (b). The textarea keeps its 96 px minimum (about three lines) as a box with the field outline, and grows with the text from 96 px where the browser supports field-sizing: content. It does not start at 56 px: an input-sized box loses the "several lines fit" cue, and without field-sizing (Safari, Firefox) the old gap would come back. The stray line under short text goes away because the outline frames the whole area (F7, "Posebnosti"). If question 1 is answered no, the textarea alone still becomes a box.

    Contrast of every changed pair

    Computed in the page from the same values the panes use (WCAG 2.2 relative luminance). Only pairs where at least one side changes are listed; "same" means that theme does not change for the pair. Text needs 4.5:1 (AA), and 7:1 where the minimum column says 7 (house target, readable outdoors); hover and press labels keep the house floor of 6:1. Outlines, focus rings, marks and chart fills need 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11). The surface rows have no WCAG minimum: they show how far apart surfaces are, which is what GH#5 is about.

    Rules this wireframe sets (system.md v0.20, after approval)

    1. Surface ladder (§3.2, §3.4, §6): dark page night.900 → card night.650 (1.44:1) → popover, muted and accent night.550 (1.85:1 against the page, 1.28:1 over a card). Light keeps paper on mist (1.15:1) and gains the edge. §3.4 "surfaces separate by tone, with no border" becomes "by tone and a 1 px edge".
    2. Edges (§6 Borders, §10.6, §10.7): a 1 px border on cards (report cards, accordion rows, dense tables, the grouped card on P9 web), bottom sheets, dialogs and menus, map frames, and the top of sticky action bars and the bottom navigation; in dark also the home closing band. Never on buttons, pills, photos or list rows.
    3. Section dividers (§5): page sections after the first start with a 1 px border line inside the content width, in the middle of the section gap (24 + 24 px mobile, 48 + 48 px web, so the gaps stay 48 and 96). Not between rows or tiles of one list.
    4. Fields (§10.3): field fill, 2 px input outline, radius 16, 56 px high (64 on single-question screens), 16 px side padding, 18 px text, label above. Hover: outline foreground. Focus: outline ring plus the 3 px ring at 2 px offset. Error: outline destructive and the message with icon below. Disabled: dashed input outline on muted, text muted-foreground, reason in the help text. Select, textarea (96 px minimum, GH#3) and search use the same box.
    5. Choice rows (§10.4): unselected rows carry the 2 px input outline on card (on a card sheet: on background). Selected: primary fill and outline, check on the right. Pills keep their fill without an outline.
    6. Secondary (§10.1, §10.2): light lagoon.200, hover lagoon.250, pressed lagoon.300. Dark lagoon.700 with a lagoon.50 label, hover lagoon.800, pressed lagoon.900: in dark the secondary goes one step darker on hover and press (a lighter step would drop the label under the 6:1 floor).
    7. Dark primary (§3.2): lagoon.250, so teal text keeps 7:1 on the lighter card; hover lagoon.200 and pressed lagoon.100 as today. ring, verified and chart-1 stay lagoon.300.
    8. Dark sidebar family (§3.2 Sidebar): the same teal as light (sidebar lagoon.900, accent and border lagoon.800), so the closing band and the portal sidebar no longer sink into the page.
    9. Dark input (§3.2): mist.400, 3:1 or more on page, card, dialog, field and muted. Light border: mist.350 (cards, dividers, table rows, chart grid).
    10. Text uses the token as is (§3.4, §13 "Do not"): no opacity modifiers on text colours (text-foreground/60, opacity-70 on text), so the §3.3 numbers hold in the build. Visual QA checks this in both themes.
    11. Principle 1 and the checklist (§1): "Space and tone, not lines and boxes" becomes "Space and tone first; a hairline where a surface ends". "No borders on cards, rows or fields (fields have an underline)" becomes "Cards, sheets and map frames have the 1 px edge; fields and choice rows have the 2 px outline; no other borders. No card inside a card."
