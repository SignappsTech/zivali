TASK-1.4GH#5v1, waiting for owner approval

Clearer contrast and section separation, light and dark; inputs that look like inputs

GitHub #5 (tester, 2026-10-19): in dark mode cards and other containers barely show against the page, sections run into each other and it is unclear which elements are inputs; light mode is better but sections still blend; in both, forms do not stand out from the text around them. This mock shows the current design (system v0.19) next to a proposal, on the home page, the lost-report form step and the report detail, plus a sheet of the components involved. Layouts and copy stay as they are; only surface colours, edges, section dividers and the field shape change.

One finding first: every text colour pair in the tokens already meets WCAG AA (table below, "Now" columns). What fails is the separation between surfaces: in dark a card is 1.10:1 against the page, a secondary button 1.25:1 against a card, the home closing band 1.04:1, and fields have no box at all. If the built app shows text paler than these numbers, that comes from the code (for example opacity modifiers such as text-foreground/60), and rule 8 below covers it.

Questions for the owner

  1. Fields as boxes: every text field, select, textarea and search becomes an outlined box (2 px input outline, field fill, radius 16) instead of a line, on web and in the app. OK? Recommended: yes. It answers "unclear which elements are inputs" and also ends GH#3 (TASK-1.3, the line sitting at different distances under the text), because there is no line any more: a textarea keeps its 96 px minimum as a box, so the space under short text is inside the box and still says "you can write several lines" (F4, "Posebnosti").
  2. Choice rows outlined too: the big option rows (Pes / Mačka, situation, the Prijavi menu) get the same 2 px outline while unselected; selected stays the teal fill with a check. OK? Recommended: yes, so everything you can pick from or type into has the same outline.
  3. Card edges: a 1 px hairline (border) on every card, sheet, map frame and sticky bar, in light and dark. Or only in dark? Recommended: both themes, one rule; light only gets crisper.
  4. Section separation: a 1 px divider line between the sections of a page, inside the content width, with the same spacing as today (drawn here). The alternative is alternating tone bands (full-width stripes in the card colour), which need more layout work. Recommended: dividers.
  5. Dark surfaces and buttons: cards one step lighter (night.750), dialogs and notes two steps lighter (night.600), secondary buttons and pills in deep teal (lagoon.700) instead of near-black teal, field outlines lighter (mist.400). OK? Recommended: yes; all numbers are in the tables below.
  6. Report detail on web: the facts and actions on the right sit in one card (F3). OK? Recommended: yes; it is the clearest group on that page.
  7. Light secondary buttons: their fill is a faint tint on the page (1.05:1; the label carries the button). Leave it, or step to lagoon.200 (1.31:1, with new hover and pressed steps)? Recommended: leave it for now; the tester found light mode fine, and changing it moves every pill and tonal button.

    What changes, in short

    1. Dark surfaces step apart. Card 1.10 to 1.23:1 against the page, dialog over card 1.18 to 1.29:1, note and track on a card 1.18 to 1.29:1.
    2. Hairline edges. Cards, sheets, map frames, sticky bars and (dark only) the home closing band get a 1 px border edge: 1.72:1 on a light card, 1.85:1 against the dark page.
    3. Section dividers. A 1 px border line in the middle of the gap between page sections. Lists keep their 20 px gaps without lines.
    4. Fields are boxes. field fill with a 2 px input outline (3:1 or more on every surface), radius 16; choice rows get the same outline.
    5. Dark secondary buttons in deep teal: 1.73:1 against a card (was 1.25:1), label 7.8:1.
    6. Light mode changes only the hairline (border mist.200 to mist.300), the field box and the new field token.

    Contrast

    Computed in the page from the same values the frames use (WCAG 2.2 relative luminance). Text pairs need 4.5:1 (AA) and the house target is 7:1 where the minimum column says 7; outlines and focus rings need 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11). The surface rows have no WCAG minimum: they show how far apart surfaces are, which is what GH#5 is about.

    Rules this wireframe sets (system.md, after approval)

    1. Surface ladder (§3.2, §3.4, §6): background → card → popover, each a visible step. Dark: card night.750, popover and muted night.600; a card is at least 1.2:1 against the dark page. Light keeps paper on mist (1.15:1) plus the edge.
    2. Edges (§6 Borders, §10.6, §10.7): a 1 px border edge on cards (report cards, accordion rows, dense tables, the grouped card on P9 web), bottom sheets, dialogs and menus, map frames, and the top of sticky action bars and the bottom navigation. In dark also the T1 closing band (in light it is dark on light already). Never on buttons, pills, photos or list rows.
    3. Section dividers (§5): page sections after the first start with a 1 px border line inside the content width, in the middle of the section gap (24 + 24 px mobile, 48 + 48 px web: the gaps stay 48 and 96). Not between rows or tiles of one list.
    4. Fields (§10.3): field fill, 2 px input outline, radius 16 (--radius), 56 px high (64 on single-question screens), 16 px side padding, 18 px text, label above. Hover: outline foreground. Focus: outline ring plus the 3 px ring at 2 px offset. Error: outline destructive and the message with icon below. Disabled: dashed input outline on muted, text muted-foreground, reason in the help text. Select, textarea and search follow the same box. Textarea (GH#3, TASK-1.3): keeps the 96 px minimum (about 3 lines) as a box; where the browser supports field-sizing: content it grows with the text from 96 px, and elsewhere it keeps 96 px with a resize handle or the rows attribute. It does not start at 56 px: an Input-sized box loses the "several lines" cue, and without field-sizing (Safari, Firefox) the old gap would come back. The floating line under short text goes away because the outline frames the whole area.
    5. Choice rows (§10.4): unselected rows carry the 2 px input outline on card (on a card sheet: on background). Selected: primary fill and outline, check on the right. Pills keep their fill without an outline.
    6. Dark secondary (§10.1, §10.2): lagoon.700 fill with a lagoon.50 label; hover lagoon.800, pressed lagoon.900, so in dark the secondary goes one step darker on hover and press (a lighter step would drop the label under the 6:1 floor for transient states).
    7. Dark input (§3.2): mist.400, 3:1 or more on page, card, dialog, field and muted (4.4:1 at the lowest, on dialogs and notes).
    8. Text uses the token as is (§3.4, §13 "Do not"): no opacity modifiers or mixes on text colours (text-foreground/60, opacity-70 on text), so the §3.3 numbers hold in the build. Visual QA checks this in both themes.
    9. Checklist (§1): "No borders on cards, rows or fields (fields have an underline)" becomes "Cards, sheets and map frames have the 1 px edge; fields and choice rows have the 2 px outline; no other borders. No card inside a card."

    Out of scope, flagged: the remaining-step bars of the flow top bar are muted (1.17:1 light, 1.59:1 dark proposed), below 3:1 for a graphic; the step count is only in the accessible name.
