Open questions for the owner

  1. Q1 Privacy: show what the AI saw on the other person's photo? The AI result of the finder's photo (C19) is not on their public report, so showing it to you is a new use of their photo, and it can say things their report does not (in the sample the finder gave no breed; the AI says "labradorec · 64 %"). A both photos, as asked: needs a line in the privacy policy (chapter 8) and the lawyer, so a content-strategist CHANGE, and perhaps a line in the found and sighting forms. B only your own photo; the other column shows what the finder published, without AI. Recommended: B, because nothing then goes beyond the public report. Switch Q1 below to see both.
  2. Q2 Photo similarity: three bands or a percentage? A bands as live today: "Fotografiji sta si zelo podobni" (or "podobni", "nekoliko podobni"). B "Podobnost fotografij: 85 %". Both carry the caveat that this is not the chance that it is your animal. Recommended: A. The number is a scaled photo distance, not a probability, but it reads like one. Its scale is still a placeholder until the real-photo run (TASK-43.7), so today's 85 % can become 70 % after the pick. Every pair above the top of the scale shows 100 %, also two different dogs that look alike. For the pre-fill you chose a percentage: there it is the model's own confidence in one attribute, which is a different kind of number. B also needs a C8 change, because today's reason carries only the band.
  3. Q3 One table instead of two? "Zakaj ta predlog?" replaces both the live reason list and the facts table (Vrsta, Velikost, Barva, Spol, Kraj, Kdaj): every factor once, with both values, whether it matches and how much it counts. Or keep the facts table under it? Recommended: one table.
  4. Q4 Strength words per factor: "odločilno" (chip), "močno", "srednje", "šibko", "ne šteje", and "pogoj" for the species (we only suggest the same species). Each word comes with a three-step meter; the mapping is in the table below. OK?
  5. Q5 Phone width: the AI panel is a closed row under the photos ("Kaj je videla umetna inteligenca"), so the verdict and the reasons stay near the top. On desktop it is open under the photos. OK?
  6. Q6 Weak matches: one line under the strength when a possible match rests on little ("Ujema se le malo podatkov …", F6) or mainly on the photos ("Predlagamo ga predvsem zaradi podobnih fotografij …", F6). OK?

    Rules this wireframe sets (v1, wait for the owner)

    1. "Zakaj ta predlog?" replaces the reason list and the facts table (Q3). One FactorRow per factor in a fixed order: Čip (when it matches), Fotografije, Vrsta, Velikost, Barva, Spol, Vzorec, Razdalja, Čas med prijavama, Čip (when it does not). The order is the same on every match, so people learn where to look.
    2. FactorRow: factor name (16 bold) and the strength on the right (meter of three 14 × 6 segments in primary, then the word, 14 bold); a second line with the verdict in bold and both values in muted-foreground ("Vaša: … · Najdena: …"); an optional caveat line (info icon 16). The word carries the meaning; the meter is aria-hidden. A description list: dt factor, dd strength, dd values.
    3. Photo row (Q2): A the band sentence, B "Podobnost fotografij: {n} %" (whole percent, no-break space). The caveat is always right under it. Below 0.25 and without vectors both variants read the same, with no number.
    4. Pair label "Najbolj podobni fotografiji" under the photos only when there is a visual reason; the most similar pair shows first (as live, TASK-43.4).
    5. AI panel: heading "Kaj je videla umetna inteligenca" with the sparkle, a table with "Vaša fotografija" and the other column (Q1), rows Vrsta, Pasma, Barva, each with a whole percentage (like the pre-fill); values below 0.6 read "ni zanesljivo". It follows the photo on screen (thumbnails, toggle). It always ends with "Predlogi umetne inteligence se lahko motijo." and "Kako to deluje?" (inline disclosure with the privacy link). Desktop: open under the photos. Phone width: a closed row (Q5).
    6. Nothing from the other report beyond its public page (Q1 B). A photo without an AI result shows one neutral line, whatever the reason (old, flagged, failed): flags (person, nsfw, no animal) and the reason are never shown. No chip numbers, no exact distance, no contact data.
    7. Weak matches (Q6): a neutral note under the strength line for a possible match that rests on one or two factors, or mainly on the photos (veto lifted or no geo-time fit, C8).
    8. Unchanged from live: comparison (side by side on desktop, toggle on phone), strength word and bars, caveat notes (sighting, chip without photo), "To je moja žival" and "Ni moja žival", "Prijavi zlorabo" in the overflow below the actions.
