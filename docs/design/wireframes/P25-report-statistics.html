Report statistics (Premium)

Wireframe of the statistics view for a report the person created (PRD FR-026 R-026.6, story TASK-117): where it opens from the owner's report page (P9), totals, per day since creation with a table alternative, shares and alerts sent by channel with Boost reach marked, the line that on-phone proximity alerts are not counted, the free owner's one quiet Premium link, the locked state after a lapse, and the organisation portal variant (free, no upgrade prompt).

Low-fi component mock in the style of the AI pre-fill wireframe, with the real tokens and components of docs/design/system.md. Web only (app later, same layout): Desktop 1200 (at 1440 the same column stays centred) and Mobile 390 (the web at phone width). Copy in the frames is Slovenian; notes are in English.

Sample report: lost dog Medo, published 3. 10. 2026, viewed on 19. 10. 2026, with a Boost from 8. to 14. 10. Numbers are invented and add up: 1.284 views, 96 shares, 1.738 alerts sent, of which 1.396 during the Boost.

Open questions for the owner

Answer by number, for example "1a, 2 ok, 3 ok …". The frames show the recommended answer; the alternative is described in the notes.

  1. What does a free owner see? (F1, F3) FR-026 gives free owners one quiet link "Statistika s Premium" on their report page. What is behind it?
    (a) A locked preview: the statistics page for this report with the block labels and a flat grey outline, no numbers at all, a short explanation and one secondary button "Več o Premium" to the plan page (P24). It is the same screen as the lapsed state, which we need anyway (AC 6).
    (b) Nothing extra: the link goes straight to the Premium plan page (P24); a free owner never sees a statistics page.
    (c) Not even the link. This needs a PRD change (R-026.6 and TASK-117 AC 2 ask for it).
    Recommended: (a). It costs little because the lapsed screen exists anyway, and it tells the owner what Premium would show for this report before they read a price. Either way: no numbers, no blurred real data, no pop-up, nothing in the report flows.
  2. Entry for a Premium owner (F1): one row "Statistika" with the three totals (1.284 ogledov · 96 deljenj · 1.738 poslanih obvestil) that opens the view, or only a text button "Statistika" next to "Uredi" and "Plakat"? Recommended: the row with the totals, so the owner sees at a glance that the report reaches people.
  3. Per-day chart (F2): one bar chart with a switch Ogledi / Deljenja / Obvestila, or three small charts one under the other? Recommended: one chart with the switch. The numbers differ a lot (one Boost day sends more alerts than a week of views), so a shared scale would flatten views and shares. The table always shows all three.
  4. Long searches: after 60 days the daily bars get very thin. Group the chart by week from then on (the table stays per day)? Recommended: yes.
  5. After a lapse (F3): show no numbers at all (as for a free owner), or keep the totals up to the lapse date visible? Recommended: no numbers. Same screen as free, simplest to build and explain; the full history comes back on resubscribing.
  6. Heatmap: you said the sightings heatmap is Premium too. It belongs to the AI search plan (R-026.8, TASK-83). Should this view link to it for an open lost report (one row at the end, drawn in F2), or keep the two apart? Recommended: the one link row here; the heatmap itself gets its own wireframe with TASK-83.
  7. Portal (F4): statistics per report only, as drawn, or also an overview across all of the organisation's reports? Recommended: per report now (R-026.6); an overview later if shelters ask for it.

    Rules this wireframe sets

    Proposed for the P25 page slice once the owner approves.

    1. Private page. Only the report's creator with Premium, and members of the owning organisation in the portal. Route proposal /r/<code>/statistika (EN /en/r/<code>/statistics), noindex. Anyone else gets the 404 page, so nobody learns whether a report has statistics. Anonymous finders have no account and so no statistics.
    2. One entry, on P9 only. Premium: the link row with the totals. Free and lapsed: the quiet link "Statistika s Premium" (16/24 muted-foreground, underlined, chart icon, 44 px target). Never in the report flows (P6, P7), their confirmations, notifications, e-mails or chat (P-2). No pop-up, no pill, no Premium colour on the free link.
    3. Order: back link, h1 "Statistika", the report row with the freshness line and "Kako štejemo?", Skupaj, Po dnevih, Po kanalih (Deljenja, Poslana obvestila), then the search plan link (open lost report, Premium only).
    4. Numbers: whole numbers with tabular-nums and the Slovenian thousands dot (1.284). No percentages, no comparison with other reports, no trend arrows, no judgement words ("dobro", "slabo").
    5. Every chart has a table alternative behind the Graf / Tabela tabs (NFR-A11Y). The chart is one role="img" with a summary (period, peak, Boost); direct labels, no legend boxes, grid lines border, bars chart-1 (system 10.9 Chart).
    6. Boost reach = alerts sent while a Boost was active (TASK-117 AC 4). Marked three ways, never by colour alone: a premium-subtle band behind those days with a labelled strip under the axis, "Povečan doseg" under the date in the table, and per alert channel a striped plum segment plus the sentence "od tega … s povečanim dosegom". The premium tonal pill "Povečan doseg · {n}" is the page's one pill (system 10.5).
    7. Proximity line under the alerts, always: alerts that the app raises on the phone by itself (FR-043) are not counted.
    8. Freshness line always ("Posodobljeno danes ob 14:20"); numbers at most 1 h old; no live refresh and no animation of the numbers.
    9. Locked (free, lapsed): no numbers on screen or in the response, one secondary button, the outline hidden from screen readers.
    10. Portal: the same blocks with no Premium words, no plan and no entitlement check (P-7).
