    Frames

    Four frames, each with Desktop 1200 and Mobile 390, and a state switch. Mobile 390 is the website at phone width; the app is not drawn (web only).

    Rules this wireframe sets

    1. Order: what stays free, then what Premium adds, then the plans (R-026.1, AC#1). On the web the two lists sit side by side with the free list on the left, first in reading and tab order; on a phone they stack.
    2. Two equal cards, yearly first (left on the web, on top on a phone). Same card, size, structure and button. The only extra on the yearly card is the tonal pill "Najboljša vrednost" (premium-subtle), the page’s one tonal pill. No toggle, no radio, nothing pre-selected, no struck-through or "was" price.
    3. The big number is what you pay (29,99 € na leto, 3,99 € na mesec). The per-month equivalent and the saving are smaller lines under it, computed from the config: per month = yearly ÷ 12, rounded to the cent (2,50 €); saving = 1 − yearly ÷ (12 × monthly), rounded down to a whole percent (37 %), with 12 × monthly in brackets (47,88 €). If the yearly plan is not cheaper, the saving line is left out.
    4. At each button, one note (14/20, muted-foreground, the button’s accessible description): price with VAT, the renewal period, cancel any time, test mode. The yearly note also names the reminder 7 days before renewal (AC#4, AC#6).
    5. Buttons: both plan buttons are primary, large and full card width, "Izberi letni paket" and "Izberi mesečni paket" (Q2). The header "Prijavi" is secondary on this page. Signed out, a plan button goes through the P16 sign-in gate and comes straight back to Checkout for that plan.
    6. A Premium user never sees plan buttons. The status card (plan, price, renewal or end date, free Boost) replaces the plans and sits under the lead. No upsell or cross-sell anywhere on the page.
    7. No pressure: no countdown, no "only today", no discount for coming back, no confirmshaming ("Obdrži Premium" is a plain text button), no ads (a payment screen, PRD §Content). Premium adds no badge (R-026.5), and the page says so.
    8. Cancelling takes no more steps than subscribing: Settings › Naročnina › "Prekliči naročnino" › one confirmation (Q4). Premium runs to the end of the paid period and the data stays.
    9. Organisation members (shelter, municipality) see no entry point, no plan and no prompt (R-026.5, AC#8). Proposal: /premium sends them to their portal home (tech lead, C15).
    10. Back from Checkout: success shows the confirming card until the entitlement is active, never the plan buttons, so nobody pays twice; cancel lands on the plans with a neutral note. Focus moves to the card or the note, announced once.
    11. Entry points (not drawn): the P8 pet limit note ("Več profilov s Premium Zaščito"), the quiet P9 "Statistika s Premium" link, the P12 "Prevedi" sheet for a free user, and Settings › Naročnina. Route /premium (EN /en/premium), noindex until Premium is live (CH-647).

    Not in this wireframe

    The app (web only for now: the app plan page with the note that checkout opens on the web, AC#7, comes with the mobile work); the yearly renewal reminder email (CH-648); the Stripe Checkout and portal pages themselves; the Premium features (report statistics TASK-117, sightings heatmap TASK-83, chat translation TASK-118).
