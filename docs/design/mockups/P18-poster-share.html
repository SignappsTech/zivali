4. A4 posters (print HTML, 210 × 297 mm, safe margin 12 mm)

Always black on white, with one mid-grey (photo well, footer rule); brand colour only in the screen preview's logo. Status is the top-band word. No phone, email, chip number, exact address or holding place. QR: 56 mm code with a 4-module white quiet zone, short link printed under it. Shown here at 50 %; print the page (A4, 100 %, no margins) to get the four posters, one per page.

Zones: top band 80 pt, one line. Top zone: photo 114 mm wide, at least 119 mm high (40 % of the page), growing into spare space up to 3:4 (152 mm), cover crop on the face; beside it the 64 mm action column: call to action 26 pt display, then the QR (56 mm code; the 8 mm gap, the margin and the link spacing keep the 4-module quiet zone white) and the short link 14 pt bold under it. Then name 72 pt; facts and "where and when" 22 pt in two columns; reward (lost only: amount 28 pt tabular, owner note 14 pt after " · "); safety line 12 pt (poster.safety_reward with a reward); footer 12 pt on the bottom edge (logo, poster.footer, the labelled vet line when the switch is on). Facts: 3 lines; 4 or 5 only while the photo keeps 119 mm. Worst case (A1: reward, note, vet line) measures a 122.7 mm photo (41 %). Omitted zones close up; the photo takes the space. PDF title "Pogrešan pes Rex – Najdimo", lang per poster.
