P17 Consent banner and preferences

Basic Consent Mode v2: nothing goes to Google before a choice, and everything starts denied. Reject, Accept and Customise look exactly the same. Nothing is switched on in advance. Changing your mind later takes one switch and Save. The banner never covers the header, the page's main action or the "Prijavi" tab. Eight frames: four web frames at 1200 px and four mobile frames (390 px app, 320 px web), then the re-ask banner and every control state.

3 × secondary md, equal widthNo primary inside the bannerNothing pre-selectedNon-modal, no scrimEscape or ignore = all denied

1. Web at 1200 px

Bottom-left from lg, 24 px from the edges, at most 560 px wide. The panel keeps one size and one place; Customise only swaps what is inside it.

Piškotki in vaša zasebnost

Nujne piškotke potrebujemo, da stran deluje, na primer za vpis. Če dovolite, z orodjem Google Analytics merimo tudi, kako se stran uporablja, brez imen, točnih lokacij in številk čipov; pred vašo odločitvijo Googlu ne pošljemo ničesar.

Politika piškotkov

role="dialog", non-modal, named "Izbira piškotkov", announced politely once. On appear, focus goes to the h2 (tabindex="-1", no ring because it is set by script); Tab continues into the link and the buttons. Order: Zavrni vse, Sprejmi vse, Prilagodi, three equal grid columns, secondary md 48. Surface popover, radius 24, shadow-lg, padding 28/24/24. h2 30/36, body 16/24 popover-foreground. The header "Prijavi" is secondary because the hero has the page's one primary. Escape closes the banner and leaves everything denied. No analytics script loads until the visitor chooses.

Nastavitve piškotkov

NujniVedno vklopljeno

Omogočajo vpis, si zapomnijo jezik, izbiro glede piškotkov in osnutke prijav ter ščitijo pred zlorabami. Ponudnika: Najdimo in Cloudflare (preverjanje, da niste robot). Trajanje je navedeno v politiki piškotkov.

Pomagajo nam razumeti, katere strani in koraki se uporabljajo, da iskanje izboljšamo. Ponudnik: Google (Google Analytics 4). Piškotka _ga in _ga_* se hranita 2 leti, podatki v Google Analytics 14 mesecev. Oglaševalskih piškotkov ne uporabljamo.

Oglase štejemo brez piškotkov in brez podatkov o vas, zato za to ne potrebujemo vašega soglasja.

"Nazaj" is a text sm button (44 px) and returns to W1 with the switches as left. Focus moves to the h2. Rows have no box: title 20/28 (Display 600; 18/28 on mobile), switch 56×32, and the whole label row is the 48 px target. Description 15/22 muted-foreground (7.8:1 light, 8.1:1 dark on popover). "Nujni" is locked on: aria-disabled, so it stays focusable and reads "vedno vklopljeno"; lock icon and word, not colour alone. Buttons: Shrani izbiro, Sprejmi vse, Zavrni vse, equal size. With nothing changed, "Shrani izbiro" saves analytics as denied. Customise is taller than the first view and may overlap page content, but its top never rises above the header (max height = viewport − 136 px: header 88 plus 24 above and below), so the header "Prijavi" always stays reachable. When the body does not fit (smaller windows, 200 % text), it scrolls and the button row stays put.

Izbira je shranjena.

Toast: popover, radius 20, shadow-pop, 15/22, check icon, 5 s, dismissible, polite. It shows only when the choice was changed from the settings. A first choice from the banner just closes the banner, with no toast and no reload. Analytics starts or stops in the same page view.

Re-ask at 1200 after a consent version change: same banner, in W1's place, with the change as its first sentence

Piškotki in vaša zasebnost

Od vaše zadnje izbire se je nekaj spremenilo: dodali smo novega ponudnika analitike. Prosimo, izberite znova.

Nujne piškotke potrebujemo, da stran deluje, na primer za vpis. Če dovolite, z orodjem Google Analytics merimo tudi, kako se stran uporablja, brez imen, točnih lokacij in številk čipov; pred vašo odločitvijo Googlu ne pošljemo ničesar.

Politika piškotkov

The banner comes back after 12 months (consent.reask_year, see M4) or when the consent version changes (consent.reask_changed with {change}, the example phrase from the content slice). That sentence is bold, so the change reads first. Until the visitor chooses again, everything is denied; the old choice does not carry over. Customise opens with the switches off.

States: the three buttons share every state (shown on one)

Switch and link

No empty or error state: the choice is stored on the device (a first-party cookie on web, local storage in the app), so there is nothing to load and nothing to fail. If storage is blocked, everything stays denied and the banner shows again on the next visit. Reduced motion: the panel and sheet fade in over 80 ms instead of sliding, and the switch thumb jumps instead of sliding.

2. Mobile

App at 390 px: a sheet at content height that docks on top of the tab bar, so the "Prijavi" tab is never covered. Web below lg: the same content as a full-width bottom sheet (M4, 320 px). The buttons stack at equal width below sm, and from sm to lg they sit in one row.
