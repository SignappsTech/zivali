P1 Web shell

The frame every web page sits in: header, footer, the three layout variants, the header popovers, the menu at 768 and 390 px, and the shared loading, empty, error, toast and banner patterns. Shell copy is final from the P1 content slice; page bodies come from P3. Two items are samples and are marked as such: the flow question (P7) and the ad slot (P21).

Nasveti za varnost

Ko se žival izgubi, smo ranljivi. Večina ljudi želi pomagati, nekateri pa poskušajo izkoristiti stisko. Ti nasveti vam pomagajo prepoznati prevaro in varno prevzeti žival.

Kako se zaščitite

  • Nikoli ne pošiljajte denarja vnaprej. Kdor je žival res našel, denarja ne zahteva.
  • Pogovor imejte v aplikaciji. Tako vaša telefonska številka ostane skrita.
  • Eno posebnost obdržite zase. V prijavi ne opišite vsega. Če nekdo trdi, da ima vašo žival, ga vprašajte za posebnost, ki je niste objavili, na primer brazgotino ali obliko lise.
  • Prosite za novo fotografijo, na primer z listkom, na katerem je današnji datum.

2. Header popovers and focus at 1200 px

"Prijavi" opens a popover of three choice rows; the avatar opens the account menu. Both open with Enter or Space, move with the arrow keys, close with Escape and return focus to their button. The skip link is the first stop in the tab order.

4. Mobile web, 390 px

Header 64 px: logo, "Prijavi" with icon and word, menu button. The menu is a full-height sheet; "Prijavi" opens a bottom sheet with the same three rows. No bottom tab bar on the web (the app has it, P2). Below 360 px the wordmark hides and the mark keeps the link name, so 320 px fits without horizontal scroll.
