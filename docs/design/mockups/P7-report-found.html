P7 Report found or sighted

No account, under a minute, from a phone. Five screens (photo, which animal, where, is it with you, publish), one question and one primary each, no sign-in prompt and no ad. The finish is a private link that works on any device. Copy from the P7 content slice v0.3; the shelter row is the shared shelter_row from P6.

A. Mobile found flow (no account)

390 px frames. Top bar: back, five bars (role="progressbar", "Korak {n} od 5"), close. Publish is bar 5 and reviews the four answers. Focus goes to the h1 on each step. The primary is lg, at the bottom, with 44 px bottom padding.

B. Sighting from a listing, and offline

"Sporoči opažanje" on a lost report (P9) starts three steps: where (P5 A3, sighting_where), when (Pravkar, Danes, Drugič; the P6 M5 choice rows), then an optional photo with a note and Send. The sighting is linked to the report and the owner is notified. Offline, a found or sighting report waits in the outbox and is sent once.

C. States that change one region (mobile, 390 px crops)

Each crop replaces the matching region of the frame named in its label; the rest of that screen stays as drawn above.

W. Web

P1 flow shell (reduced header, minimal footer), centred 560 px column, the bars in a row under the header. Step 1 is a file input with a drop area ("Izberi fotografijo", "ali jo povlecite sem"); step 3 opens P5 E1 as a content dialog; step 5 is M5 in the column. Enter advances; errors are announced.
