P6 Report lost

One question per screen, no ad and no payment prompt. A new pet takes seven steps (pet, photos, details, where, when, what happened, review); a saved pet takes five (pet, where, when, what happened, review). The bars always show the real count. The draft is saved on every step and survives the sign-in gate. Copy from the P6 content slice v0.4.

A. Mobile flow

390 px frames. Top bar: back, one bar per step (role="progressbar", "Korak {n} od {total}"), close. Focus goes to the h1 on every step change. "Naprej" is lg, sticky at the bottom with 44 px bottom padding; tall frames show the whole scroll length.

C. States that change one region (mobile, 390 px crops)

Each crop replaces the matching region of the frame named in its label; everything else on that screen stays as drawn above.

W. Web

P1 flow shell (reduced header, minimal footer). Steps 1 to 6 sit in the centred 560 px column with the bars in a row under the header and the primary at the bottom (step 4 is P5 E1, photos use "Izberi fotografije" with "ali jih povlecite sem"). On lg the review widens to summary left and photos right. Enter advances; errors are announced.
